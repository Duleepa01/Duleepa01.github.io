03 Learning Notes

Notes.

Things I picked up from project work and coursework.

2026 · From project work engineering
Database schema changes mid-project are expensive
Reworked the schema partway through the Pharmacy system. Foreign key constraints, data migration, cascading updates - all more involved than expected. Getting the design right upfront saves a lot of time later.
databaseslessons-learned
2026 · From psychology studies psychology
Validation messages get ignored more than you'd expect
During testing on BeverageHub, users consistently missed validation errors in the checkout flow. The messages were accurate but not placed where users were looking. Moved them inline and the issue went away.

Requirements take longer than I used to think
I used to treat requirements gathering as a quick step before the real work. After a few projects, I spend more time on it. Unclear requirements show up later as bugs that are hard to explain.
